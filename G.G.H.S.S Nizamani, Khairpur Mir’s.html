Who we are

About Our School

Government Girls Higher Secondary School Nizamani, Khairpur Mir’s provides education for girls up to Class 12. We aim to prepare every student with confidence, good character and a bright future.

Our Mission

To give every girl quality education in a safe and respectful environment.

Our Vision

Confident, educated women who contribute to their families and community.
